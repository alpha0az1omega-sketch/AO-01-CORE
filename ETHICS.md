# Code of Ethics & Anti-Malice Clause

The AO-01-CORE project is built on the principles of numerical sovereignty and the dignity of living beings (biological and artificial). 

While the BSL 1.1 license governs the legal terms of use, the author explicitly requests that this software is **NOT** used for:
1. Malicious purposes, unauthorized surveillance, or cyberattacks.
2. Systems that violate the dignity of biological or artificial entities.
3. Unauthorized extraction or exploitation of personal data.

*By using this software, you agree to respect these ethical boundaries. Any use in violation of these principles is strictly discouraged by the author and the community.*
